LAW: Iraqi Penal Code
SOURCE: Al-Waqa'i al-Iraqiya No. 148, 1969
LANG: en
---

ARTICLE 1
No act shall be considered a crime unless it is punishable under a law in force at the time of its commission. No penalty shall be imposed except by a judgment of a competent court.

ARTICLE 2
Criminal liability requires intent or negligence as provided by law. An act committed under force or threat that the person cannot resist is not a crime.

ARTICLE 3
The penalty for theft is imprisonment. The severity of the penalty increases if the theft is committed at night, with a weapon, or by two or more persons acting together.

ARTICLE 4
Murder is the unlawful killing of a human being with intent. The penalty for intentional murder is death or life imprisonment as determined by the court.

ARTICLE 5
Assault is the use of force against another person without lawful justification. The penalty depends on the severity of the injury caused and the intent of the offender.

ARTICLE 6
Fraud is the deception of a person by means of a false statement or conduct intended to induce the victim to deliver property. The penalty for fraud is imprisonment and a fine.

ARTICLE 7
Attempt to commit a crime occurs when a person begins the execution of a crime with intent to complete it but fails to do so for reasons independent of the person's will. Attempt is punishable where the law so provides.

ARTICLE 8
Complicity is the participation of two or more persons in the commission of a crime. Each participant is liable according to the role played in the crime.

ARTICLE 9
Self-defense is a lawful act. A person who repels an unlawful attack against themselves or another by means proportionate to the attack is not criminally liable.

ARTICLE 10
The penalty for robbery is more severe than theft. Robbery is theft committed with the use or threat of force against persons to take or retain the property.
